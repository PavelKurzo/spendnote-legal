See where your money goes

Log an expense in seconds, keep a private savings note, and use Spaces so everyone can see who paid and who owes. SpendNote is a record-keeping tool — not a bank, and it does not convert currencies.

What you can do

The same tools that live in the app.

Expenses

Amount-first entry with categories, receipts, an optional place, and a split that can stay personal or go into a Space.

Savings

A private note of money you set aside or take out. It is not a bank balance, and it is not counted as spending.

Spaces

Shared costs, who paid, equal or custom splits, balances, and a record of settlements. SpendNote does not move money.

Insights

Totals by category and period. Each currency is counted on its own, so nothing is converted behind the scenes.

Home Screen widgets

Type an amount on the widget and add it without opening the app. If you were offline, it syncs when you come back.

Who pays?

A local spinner for deciding in the moment. It stays on your device and does not set who paid a shared expense.

How SpendNote works

A few things the app is careful about.

Not a bank

Savings and settlements are notes you keep. SpendNote never holds or transfers money.

No currency conversion

Amounts stay in the currency you enter. Shared balances only include that Space’s currency.

Delete anytime

Sign in with email or Apple. Delete Account in Profile permanently removes your SpendNote data.
