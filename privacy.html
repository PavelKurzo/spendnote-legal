Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Pavel Kurzo (“I”, “me”) handles information in SpendNote, an iOS app for tracking personal spending and splitting shared costs.

Who is responsible

SpendNote is operated by Pavel Kurzo. For privacy questions, email pavel.kurzo@mail.ru.

What SpendNote collects

The app stores the information you choose to enter, plus what is needed to run your account:

  • Account. Display name, email address, and a password handled by the sign-in service. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may share a name, email (or a private relay email), and an Apple user identifier.
  • Expenses. Amount, currency, title, category, date, optional note, optional place name and map coordinates, who paid, how the cost is split, and which Space it belongs to.
  • Receipt photos. Images you attach from the camera or photo library.
  • Savings. Amounts you mark as set aside or taken out, with currency, date, and an optional note. This is a personal note, not a bank balance.
  • Spaces. Space name, currency, icon, members, email invites, and settlement records between members.
  • Categories and preferences. Custom categories, preferred currency, widget category choices, and language. Language is stored on your device.

Information from your device

  • Camera and photos are used only if you attach a receipt.
  • Location is used only if you add a place to an expense, to suggest nearby names. SpendNote does not track you in the background.
  • Home Screen widgets keep a small copy of widget settings and queued quick-add amounts in an app group on your device so the widget can work when the app is closed.
  • Who pays? spinner stays on your device. It is a local coin-flip and is not stored on the server.

How the information is used

  • To create and keep your account.
  • To show your expenses, savings, Spaces, balances, and insights.
  • To let people you invite join a Space, using the email you enter.
  • To send a password reset link if you ask for one.
  • To operate widgets and sync a quick expense when you open the app.

SpendNote does not show ads, does not sell your data, and does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Who can see shared data

Personal expenses and savings are visible only to you. If you add an expense to a Space, members of that Space can see it, including amount, splits, and any receipt or place you attach. People you invite by email can join that Space once they have a SpendNote account with that same email.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts authentication, the database, and receipt file storage.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store distribution, MapKit place search, and on-device services.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy. GitHub may log technical data such as IP address when you open these pages.

Those providers process data on my behalf to run SpendNote. SpendNote does not convert currencies and is not a payment or banking service.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. In the app, Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your account, personal expenses, savings, Spaces you own, receipt photos, and related records. Shared expenses you created may also disappear for other members. Some residual copies can remain in backups for a limited time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to certain processing. The fastest way to delete everything SpendNote stores for you is Delete Account in the app. You can also email pavel.kurzo@mail.ru.

International transfers

Servers and subprocessors may be outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on the provider’s contractual safeguards.

Children

SpendNote is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the updated date at the top will change. Continue using SpendNote after an update means you accept the revised policy.
